# Business English Pro - Platform Architecture & Content Strategy

## Overview
Business English Pro is a comprehensive CEFR-aligned (A1-C2) learning platform designed for professionals. It integrates AI tutoring, skill-specific modules (Reading, Listening, Writing, Speaking), and enterprise management features.

## Core User Journeys
1. **The Explorer (Visitor):** Arrives on landing page, views pricing, takes placement test.
2. **The Learner (Student):** Uses dashboard to track progress, completes lessons in specific skill modules, interacts with AI Tutor.
3. **The Instructor (Teacher):** Manages groups, reviews student submissions, builds custom content.
4. **The Supervisor (Admin):** Monitors platform health, manages users/payments, and content analytics.

## Initial Screen List
1. **Landing Page:** High-conversion hero, statistics, testimonials, and pricing tiers.
2. **Student Dashboard:** Personalized overview with XP, streaks, and "Continue Learning" shortcuts.
3. **Course Catalog (CEFR levels):** Navigation by level and skill (Reading, Writing, etc.).
4. **Lesson View (Reading/Writing):** Immersive learning interface with AI explanation and progress tracking.
5. **AI Tutor / Speaking Lab:** Dedicated interface for real-time AI conversation and speaking analysis.
6. **Student Profile & Certificates:** Achievement gallery and QR-verified certificate management.

## Technical Foundation
- **UI:** React/Next.js/Tailwind with Shadcn-inspired components.
- **Motion:** CSS-based smooth transitions and hover states.
- **Content:** Automated educational content generation following CEFR standards.
